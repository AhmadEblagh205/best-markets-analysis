# Audit methodology and decision boundaries

- Unit: respondent, not customer or transaction. No survey weights or national population frame. Interest matching uses the original case-insensitive substring rule; it is a proxy for product fit, not verified purchase intent. Residence strings are stripped; ambiguous labels are not automatically geocoded. Missing role records cannot enter eligibility; unknown residence is reported separately. No respondent identifiers are available in the analysis schema, so duplicate people cannot be established or deduplicated safely.
- Spend proxy: total reported learning expenditure / months programming; assumes spending is spread across programming tenure. Zero months becomes 1 by convention, not measured duration. Half-month sensitivity tests that convention. No currency/PPP or inflation adjustment is attempted; the historical nominal-dollar fields follow original source interpretation. Missing/invalid values are excluded only from spend statistics. All valid values, including high and zero values, enter the primary report.
- Focus countries: top four by known-residence interested-respondent counts, ties alphabetically. Count share is divided by all eligible known-country respondents, not only top four or valid spenders. Country groups with no valid spend remain visible with undefined estimates and [0,1] missingness bounds.
- ECDF F(x) counts observations <= x; share >= p is 1-F(p-) rather than 1-F(p) in the presence of ties. Use exact comparisons, not interpolated plot reading. Log-like axis retains zero.
- Mean is sensitive to a long right tail; median describes the middle observation, not expected expenditure or conversion. Deletion conditions on spend <= cap; capping changes the outcome but keeps the denominator. Both change the estimand; neither is a factual cleaning rule. Sensitivity grid is illustrative, not data-derived. Bootstrap medians use 2,000 iid within-country resamples (seed 0), percentile endpoints; no guarantee of nominal coverage under ties, selection or dependence. No population inference or significance claim.
- Missingness bounds use known group count N, valid count n and k threshold successes. With m=N-n unknown binary outcomes, total success lies between k and k+m, hence exact finite-cohort bounds. Complete-case estimates can be biased under differential response. Bounds are not sampling intervals or a remedy for coverage error.
- Controlled experiments: RNG seed 2026, 100 independent simulated samples of 500 per group; A has rare high valid spending, B moderate spending. Compare raw vs deletion vs cap at 500. Expected raw means A=86.4, B=40; conditional trimmed A=6.9565, B=40; capped A=46.4, B=40. For A selective observation depends on true threshold status; known realised share enables direct error and exact-bound check. Expected full share .40; asymptotic complete-case share .16/(.16+.54)=.22857. This deliberately constructed stress case is not a model of the FCC survey. No confidence level is attached to the 100-world bound count; containment is algebraic.
- Real data: absent from supplied archive; download failed due to network access and connector size limits. Legacy outputs and README preserved, not reproduced. Re-run real CSV commands later; verify source/version/hash, exclusion counts, threshold sensitivity and any historical differences before public promotion. Do not infer current demand, actual conversion, advertising ROI or national earnings.
